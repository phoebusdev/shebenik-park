## 🎨 Design Highlights

### Why Earth Tones?
- Authentic to mountain landscape
- Found in Byzantine heritage sites
- Never feel dated or trendy
- Create warm, welcoming feeling
- Appropriate for cultural heritage

### Why Montserrat?
- Geometric, modern sans-serif
- Clear personality for headings
- Creates visual hierarchy
- Works at all sizes
- Complements Inter body text

### Why Keep Inter for Body?
- Proven screen readability
- Already optimized
- Creates clear contrast with Montserrat
- Professional appearance
- No extra file load
