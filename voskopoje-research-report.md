**Detailed Findings:**
- 1769: Initial attack and pillaging by Muslim Albanian bands (pro-Russian sentiment/Orlov Revolt involvement)
- 1769-1788: Series of escalating raids
- 1788: Final razing by Ali Pasha's forces - town "practically destroyed"
- Consequence: Commerce shifted to Korçë and Berat; survivors emigrated to Thessaly and Macedonia

### Printing Press - VERIFIED with CLARIFICATION ✓✓
| Claim | Research Finding | Status |
|-------|-----------------|--------|

**Important Clarification:** The printing press was the **second in Ottoman Europe** (after Constantinople), making it the **first in Ottoman Balkans outside Constantinople**. Some sources state 19 books total, others 21 works between 1731-1769. Timeline discrepancy exists (1720 founding vs. 1731-1769 printing period).

- **Established:** 1743

- **Significance:** "Only Christian university in Ottoman Empire"; "worthy jewel of the city"
